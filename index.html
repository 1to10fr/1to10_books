How to get the most from them

  • Type it yourselfType every example instead of copying it. Your fingers learn what your eyes skim past.
  • Try before you peekAnswer each check and practice question before you open the answer key.
  • Finish the challengesComplete a chapter's challenges before you move on to the next one.
